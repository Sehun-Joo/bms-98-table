How the ranges and rankings work

Nearby difficulty values are first grouped into 0.05-wide bands. Adjacent bands are combined to get close to 15 charts, without splitting a band to hit a quota. Ranges include their lower bound and exclude their upper bound; displayed chart values are rounded. This produces 68 folders of 9–23 charts.

The ranking uses 7.7 million personal bests from 35,845 players, with player ability adjustment and a 98%-specific scoring model. The order is unchanged from the first release. Read the method review and additional validation.

Popular charts may be practiced more. Plays/player is an aggregate effort indicator, not a correction. These data cannot estimate equal-practice difficulty or next-attempt success. “Supported” means sufficient observation counts, not certainty.
