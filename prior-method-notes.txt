INSANE 98% DIFFICULTY TABLE

1,035 charts, sorted easiest to hardest by estimated 98% EX personal-best difficulty.
86 folders: 3 folders of 13 charts, 83 folders of 12 charts. Lower numbers are easier.
This table contains chart references; it does not include song/chart downloads.

USE
beatoraja / compatible forks: close the game, copy insane-98.bmt into its existing
table directory, then restart. The table is named "Insane 98% — easiest to hardest".
Do not replace any existing table files. Compatibility was checked against the
beatoraja TableData format; the artifact has not been imported into your running game.

Web/GLAssist-style table: index.html, header.json and data.json form a standard
BMSTable package. Host these three files together and register the index.html URL
in your table importer. This package has not been publicly hosted.

Read index.html directly in a browser to inspect all ranks and effort indicators.
rankings.json contains exact model estimates and support counts.

METHOD
Selected model: target98 (selection criterion: near-target log loss).
Players used: 35,845; PBs used: 7,697,372.
Held-out results: 1,155,568; near-target holdout: 23,508.
Three candidates were evaluated: a shared-slope score model, a chart-specific-slope
score model, and 98%-specific logistic calibration of the chart-slope model.
All adjust for player ability estimated from overlapping chart results.
Selection uses held-out log loss near the target, not training fit.
See validation.json for both overall and near-target metrics and the raw-rate baseline.
One 15% holdout was used for model selection; its score is a validation result,
not an independent final test set or proof that causal selection bias is removed.

Exact target: 100*EX >= 98*maximum_EX. Display percentages are not rounded first.
Input devices are pooled. Each player contributes one PB per chart, not one row
per attempt. Accounts with fewer than 10 retained Insane chart results are excluded.
Flagged cheat records, nonpositive/impossible scores, and records with a nonmodal
maximum EX score for that chart are excluded. No clear-lamp requirement is imposed.

Latent scoring model: -log((maximum_EX-EX+0.5)/(maximum_EX+0.5)) = chart intercept
+ positive chart slope * player ability + residual. Huber fitting reduces influence
of abandoned or stale low PBs; ridge penalties stabilize sparse players and slopes.
Player ability is standardized. Logistic calibration, when selected, estimates
98%-specific chart thresholds and slopes, regularized toward the score model.
Chart difficulty is the estimated ability at the 50% 98%-PB achievement point.
For the score model it is the ability at the modeled score-location crossing 98%.
Ability units are internal standardized units, not the original star difficulty.

UNCERTAINTY AND PRACTICE
366 charts are marked provisional: fewer than 100 usable records, fewer
than 5 recorded 98% achievements, or fewer than 30 players within one ability unit
of the estimated threshold. 91 charts have no recorded 98% achievement in the
modeling cohort: their ordering is extrapolated, not evidence of achieved 98%.
All charts remain in the sorted folders; provisional status is in the readable
ranking and standard table comments. "Supported" is a count-based support flag,
not a posterior confidence interval. Small rank differences are not precise.

POPULARITY: total plays / total players is shown as an effort indicator using
the archive's chart aggregates. Those aggregates are not restricted to the model's
filtered cohort. Popularity alone is not penalized. Repeated practice may improve
PBs, even though repeated attempts are not counted as independent observations.
Complete per-player/chart attempt histories and PB dates are unavailable, so the
model cannot rank charts under equal practice budgets or predict attempts needed.
This is a historical PB ranking, not a next-attempt probability or current-skill rating.
Selection by chart preference, input device, score age, and specialist skill may remain.

REPRODUCE
The reproduce directory contains the exact script, manifest, and dependency pins.
Install requirements.txt in a Python environment, then run:
python build_table.py --db "PATH/TO/lr2ir-archive-v3.db" --out "PATH/TO/new-table"
The source database is read-only and is not included in this package.
test_model.py checks recovery with simulated ability-dependent chart selection.
