AAA, MAX− and 98% difficulty tables

These are three independently fitted target-achievement rankings of the same 1,035 Insane charts. They share a latent scoring-ability scale estimated from actual player scores. Each target then gets its own chart-specific logistic thresholds and slopes. A chart can move relative to other charts when the target changes.

Exact targets

  • AAA: EX/max ≥ 8/9 (88.888…%). Integer test: 9 × EX ≥ 8 × max.
  • MAX−: EX/max ≥ 17/18 (94.444…%). Integer test: 18 × EX ≥ 17 × max.
  • 98%: EX/max ≥ 98/100. Integer test: 100 × EX ≥ 98 × max.

No rounded displayed percentage is used to classify success. MAX− uses the common IIDX-style milestone selected for this table. It is not the 26/27 target that beatoraja labels MAX− in its target menu.

Validation for the new targets

Chart parameters were fitted on 80% of players. For held-out players, half their scores estimated ability; the other half were evaluated, requiring at least five reference charts. The near-target group was defined using training-only score-model parameters. Lower log loss is better. This reuses the established evaluation design; it is not an untouched independent test of every possible modeling choice.

Ranges and uncertainty

Nearby fitted difficulty values are grouped in small value bands, then neighboring bands are combined toward 15 charts without splitting a band for a quota. Band width is selected from 0.01, 0.025 and 0.05 to put the average near 15; the original 98% folders are unchanged. Range bounds are inclusive below and exclusive above. DAAA, DMAX− and D98 are estimated ability thresholds, not EX percentages or ★ levels.

For the new targets, a chart is provisional if there are fewer than 100 usable PBs, fewer than five successes or five misses, or fewer than 30 players within one ability unit of its threshold. The unchanged 98% flags use the original rule. These are support checks, not confidence intervals. Cross-target threshold crossings found: 0. Models are fitted independently; poorly supported estimates are not automatically forced to agree.

Practice amount, age of PBs, individual strengths and input devices remain potential biases. Plays/player is an aggregate indicator, not a correction. The archive does not support an equal-attempt-budget difficulty ranking or next-attempt probabilities.
